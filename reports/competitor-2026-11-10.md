# Crayon Cove Press: competitive comparison (2026-11-10)

## Verdict

**The covers aren't plain.** All four are bright, character-led and readable at thumbnail size, and they're as good as or better than most indie competitors. **The two coloring books aren't plain inside either.** They have 50 varied, cute pictures with thick lines, single-sided pages and hollow word labels, which is genre-standard or better. **What looks plain is the two puzzle books' interiors and what every book offers for the money.** The Christmas Activity Book has 40 puzzles across only 3 activity types in 56 pages. Its dot-to-dots are generic shapes (fish, cat, dinosaur, rocket), not Christmas pictures, and nothing inside is illustrated. Competitors in that niche give about 100–120 pages and 6–10 activity types at a similar price. The Mazes book has 60 bare grid mazes, while the leading maze books offer 101 mazes on 110–120 pages, often with themed art. All four books are $9.99 against rivals that mostly offer twice the count for the same price or less, and ours have 0 reviews. **Fix the activity book first, because its Christmas selling window closes in about 5 weeks. Then add count badges to every cover and reconsider price or page count.**

> **Data caveat:** Amazon blocked all scripted fetches (HTTP 503). The one live Amazon US search snapshot I captured is for *Christmas bold & easy*, before I was told to stop using the browser. Ratings, review counts and BSR for the other three niches come from search snippets and third-party retailers, and most of those figures could not be verified. They're marked ⚠ where uncertain.
