# Crayon Cove Press: author bio (Amazon Author Central)

Crayon Cove Press makes bold, simple colouring books for little hands: big pictures, thick lines and one picture per page, so first-time colourers can enjoy every page. Based in Australia, we create themed books kids love, including trucks, Christmas, dinosaurs and Aussie animals, plus cozy and detailed books for older colourers.
